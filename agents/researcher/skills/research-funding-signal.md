# Skill: Research Funding Signal

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-signal-discovery.md`, `tech-macro-regime.md`, `/research-funding-signal`

Use this skill to collect funding, acquisition, partnership, hiring, and enterprise buying signals that may indicate durable market interest.

## Output

| Signal | Organization | Type | Evidence | Source ID | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [signal] | [org] | [funding / M&A / partnership / hiring / procurement] | [evidence] | SRC-001 | [High / Medium / Low] |

## Quality Rule

Treat funding as a signal, not proof of product-market fit.
