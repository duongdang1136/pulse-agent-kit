# Skill: Research Product Signal

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-signal-discovery.md`, `tech-trend-confirmation.md`, `/research-product-signal`

Use this skill to collect product evidence: launches, pricing, packaging, integrations, customer stories, roadmap movement, and feature adoption.

## Output

| Signal | Product / Vendor | Evidence | Source ID | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [signal] | [name] | [evidence] | SRC-001 | [High / Medium / Low] |

## Quality Rule

Prefer primary launch notes, docs, customer case studies, and pricing pages over commentary.
