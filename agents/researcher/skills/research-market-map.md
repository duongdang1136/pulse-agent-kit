# Skill: Research Market Map

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-signal-discovery.md`, `tech-trend-confirmation.md`, `/research-market-map`

Use this skill to map vendors, tools, competitors, alternatives, categories, and ecosystem positions around a technology domain.

## Output

| Category | Player / Tool | Positioning | Evidence | Source ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [category] | [name] | [leader / challenger / niche / emerging] | [evidence] | SRC-001 |

## Notes

- Separate category movement from vendor marketing.
- Mark missing evidence as `Unknown`; do not infer market leadership from a single source.
