# Skill: Durability Check

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-trend-confirmation.md`

Use this skill to decide whether a trend is likely to persist or is mostly short-term hype.

## Checks

- Repeated independent signals across time and source types.
- Clear problem-solution fit.
- Ecosystem and integration growth.
- Switching cost or structural advantage.
- Hype risk, vendor narrative, shallow content volume.
- Invalidation conditions.

## Output

| Factor | Evidence | Source ID | Durability Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| [factor] | [evidence] | SRC-001 | [positive / neutral / negative] |

**Durability verdict:** [durable / watchlist / hype-risk / weak]
