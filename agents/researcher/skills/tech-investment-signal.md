# Skill: Tech Investment Signal

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-macro-regime.md`, `tech-signal-discovery.md`  
**Output section:** Investment Signal

Use this skill to identify capital, hiring, partnership, acquisition, and budget movement around a technology domain.

## Signal Types

- Funding and acquisitions.
- Vendor launches and cloud marketplace movement.
- Hiring demand and role specialization.
- Partnerships and enterprise programs.
- Budget narrative in analyst reports or procurement content.

## Output

| Signal | Type | Direction | Source ID | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [signal] | [funding / hiring / partnership / acquisition / budget] | [up / flat / down] | SRC-001 | [notes] |

**Investment verdict:** [tailwind / neutral / headwind / unknown]
