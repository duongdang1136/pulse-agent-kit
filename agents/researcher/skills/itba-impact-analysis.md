# Skill: ITBA Impact Analysis

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-trend-confirmation.md`, `tech-trend-summary.md`, `Research-Report.md`

Use this skill to translate research findings into impacts for ITBA handoff without turning recommendations into approved requirements.

## Impact Areas

- Scope and out-of-scope.
- Functional requirements.
- API, integration, and schema assumptions.
- UX workflow and screen behavior.
- Logging, metrics, and analytics.
- Risk, compliance, and open questions.

## Output

| BA Area | Impact | Evidence Source | Recommendation | Needs ITBA Decision? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [area] | [impact] | SRC-001 | [recommendation] | [Yes / No] |
