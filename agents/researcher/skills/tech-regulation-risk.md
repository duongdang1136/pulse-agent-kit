# Skill: Tech Regulation Risk

**Agent:** Researcher  
**Used by:** `tech-macro-regime.md`, `tech-trend-confirmation.md`  
**Output section:** Regulation and Risk

Use this skill to assess compliance, legal, privacy, security, policy, and governance pressure around a technology domain.

## Checks

- Legal or regulatory constraints.
- Security, privacy, safety, or governance requirements.
- License and IP concerns.
- Data residency or industry compliance.
- Vendor lock-in and procurement risk.

## Output

| Risk | Evidence | Severity | Source ID | ITBA Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [risk] | [evidence] | [High / Medium / Low] | SRC-001 | [impact] |

**Risk verdict:** [acceptable / needs mitigation / blocks adoption / unknown]
